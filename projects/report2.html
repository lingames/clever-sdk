Drawing on experience from mobile applications (APPs), when advertisers postback middle funnel events, it can raise the return on ad spend (ROAS) by 10 - 20%. Benefits of postback middle funnel events - Signal Enhancement: Gain a more comprehensive understanding of users' full-path behaviors and provide the model with more comprehensive advertising data. - Performance Improvement: As more data is incorporated into the model, accurate predictions can be made, thereby improving the final advertising performance. Background Mini-programs are built-in applications developed based on the TikTok APP. Users can directly play different types of games and watch a variety of short dramas within TikTok, enjoying an entertainment feast more conveniently. Since the development of the application and the built-in data logging are both set according to the advertisers' habits and the data is sent back to the platform. To more efficiently assist TikTok in collecting relevant advertising signal data, TikTok hereby supports the function of advertisers sending back intermediate events based on the existing JS SDK. Developers can directly call the JS SDK in the early stage of game development and send back intermediate events according to the instructions, ultimately helping to optimize the model and improve the final effect of advertising placement. Benefits of Sending Back Intermediate Events The Impact Path of Signals on Advertising Effect The specific improvement in effect varies from app to app. The iteration of the model takes time, so the short-term benefits may not be obvious, but the long-term benefits are relatively certain. - Funnel Optimization: Completing the logging and sending back of intermediate events provides an optimization basis for user link monitoring and the improvement of advertising monetization efficiency. - Signal Enhancement: A more comprehensive understanding of the user's full-path behavior provides more comprehensive advertising data for the model. - Effect Improvement: As more data is incorporated into the model, accurate predictions can be made, improving the final advertising effect.
